Third-pass model audit · why the numbers move

These are sensitivity tests of the same assumptions, not observations. The event presets do not automatically match the selected calendar date. Full occupancy is an explicit what-if override.

Downtown Chatham · documented activity evidence (2017–2026)

Select a documented event pattern above to test how an event-driven demand scenario changes the seven-hour forecast. Published figures refer to entire events, weekends or theatre seasons—not hourly occupancy in bars. The model uses explicitly assumed demand multipliers; it is not statistically calibrated to these reports. Event selection is manual, not automatic detection. A festival visitor is not necessarily a bar customer.

  • 2017 RetroFest: organizer estimated up to 30,000 visitors over two days. Source
  • 2024 Capitol Theatre: more than 50,000 patrons across nearly 195 events reported by October (not an hourly count). Source
  • 2022 RetroFest: estimated 20,000–30,000 visitors over the weekend. Source
  • 2025 Crowfest: organizers anticipated approximately 25,000–30,000 visitors on the second and third days, weather permitting (forecast, not measured attendance). Source
  • 2025 RetroFest: organizers projected 30,000+ attendance (forecast, not final count). Source
  • 2026 Capitol Theatre: 55,000 attendance across 153 shows/events from September to April (seasonal total). Source

Evidence supports the existence and relative scale of major activity peaks, not precise hourly venue fill, networking opportunities, or a statistically validated probability. No saving, import or export.
