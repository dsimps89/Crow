Cast your City Signal vote

Click one stage box to register your vote using the settings on the left. Each stage box registers a vote. You may update settings at any time and vote again when the 24-hour lock ends. Every Friday at 8 PM (Chatham time), the weekly totals restart.

The original uploaded night-stage descriptions are included below each stage when selected. Changes to inputs update the preview; they do not submit or alter an existing vote.

Vote strength = 100 × votes for the leading stage ÷ total votes in the current weekly cycle on this browser (when votes exist). Capacity signal = 100 × entered occupancy ÷ 100, capped at 100. A 100/100 score is possible, but it is not a verified crowd count. Unlimited potential votes require a shared server; this standalone file only sees votes made in this browser.

Privacy: voting inputs and timestamp are stored only in this browser's localStorage. The submitted input snapshot is discarded after 24 hours; weekly stage totals reset Friday at 8 PM. Clearing browser storage removes votes immediately. No data is sent to a server. Browser storage is not tamper-proof and does not prevent voting from another device or private window.
