Model assumptions

Uncalibrated scenario model: the 38/52/10 crowd-fill weights, time curve, venue capacities and randomness are assumptions, NOT fitted to Chatham activity records. Set capacity fill to 100% to simulate every venue full, and adjust Networking sensitivity (50–200%) to test stronger or weaker Networking response. The displayed 10–90% range describes model noise, not empirically measured accuracy. The crow gauge measures fit with your preferred intensity, not an actual chance of meeting someone.

Cast your City Signal vote

Click one stage box to register your vote using the settings on the left. Each stage box registers a vote. You may update settings at any time and vote again when the 24-hour lock ends. Stage selection immediately submits one vote. Every Friday at 8 PM (Chatham time), the public voting cycle restarts.

Signal strength—

Vote strength = 100 × votes for the leading stage ÷ total votes in the current shared weekly cycle (when votes exist). Capacity signal = 100 × entered occupancy ÷ 100, capped at 100. A 100/100 score is possible, but it is not a verified crowd count. Votes are shared across devices through Firebase when configured. Vote share is not measured attendance.

Privacy: when Firebase is configured, votes and an optional input snapshot are submitted to the shared backend. Snapshots are scheduled for deletion using Firestore TTL (deletion may be delayed); the server enforces one vote per anonymous account per 24 hours. Anonymous accounts are not strong proof of one unique person.
