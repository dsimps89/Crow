Data, privacy, limits & deployment

Backend saves aggregate venue counts, server timestamps and observation methods. No visitor identifiers or precise locations are recorded. Counts expire from the public current-signal view after 24 hours; historical aggregate reports remain available for calibration. All capacities are speculative planning assumptions, doubled from previous working midpoints and not legal occupancy limits. Backend needs hosting and persistent storage; a static HTML host alone cannot provide live shared updates.
