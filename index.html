How the community pulse works

The forecast blends opening hours, day of week, calendar dates, seasonal weather assumptions, entertainment, promotions and synthetic event surges. The forecast is recalculated every 25 minutes and animated between cycles. The featured event you enter is saved locally in this browser, not automatically published to other visitors.
