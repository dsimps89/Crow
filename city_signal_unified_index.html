How the model works · precision & sources

The activity curve uses an assumed daily peak near 10:30 PM, weekday factors, weather, events, community activity and venue fill. Estimated people = assumed venues × assumed capacity × modeled fill. The 80% range uses a ±20% relative scenario variation (not a statistically calibrated prediction interval). Spread radius = √(0.35² + 2 × D × elapsed hours), where D = 0.035 km²/hour × an activity factor. This radius describes a conceptual spatial kernel, not a measured walking distance or GIS heatmap. No real pedestrian, business-opening, venue-capacity or event-attendance feeds are connected.

Precision requires time-stamped observed footfall or venue counts. Compare predictions against observations using MAE, RMSE, bias and interval coverage before claiming accuracy. The earlier business atlas is an incomplete inventory and surname counts cannot identify current attendance.
